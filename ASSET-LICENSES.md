**Not in the public pack** (free to use in your videos, but their licences forbid re-sharing the files). Get them yourself:

| Folder | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Music, Footage, Sound effects / Mixkit | `app\python\python.exe app\tools\fetch_mixkit_assets.py` downloads them from Mixkit (about 2.6 GB). [Mixkit licence](https://mixkit.co/license/) |
| Sound effects / Sonniss GDC 2026 | download the free GDC bundle from [sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc](https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc) and unzip it into that folder |
| Icons / Remixicon | [remixicon.com](https://remixicon.com) (Remix Icon License) |

Arabic memes, Brands (client logos) and Voice samples from the author's own setup are never shared.
In the app, **⚙ Settings > Use assets that can't be shared** decides whether Claude and Codex may use these restricted folders.
